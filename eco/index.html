01 / THE SCIENCE AND ENGINEERING

Evidence follows the contamination pathway.

A source–pathway–receptor model: mine pressure can alter runoff or discharge; changes may reach water and habitats; ecological surveys show longer-term biological response. Correlation alone does not establish cause.

01 / SOURCE

Mining pressure

Extraction or material movement, exposed ground, tailings and altered drainage.

02 / PATHWAY

Water signal

pH, conductivity, turbidity and temperature; targeted metals testing by a laboratory.

03 / LAND

Satellite change

Sentinel-2 vegetation and surface-water indices; rainfall and seasonal comparisons.

04 / RECEPTOR

Life responds

Bird acoustics, pollinator transects and vegetation plots over repeated surveys.

05 / RESPONSE

Verify & act

Prioritise field visits, sample the suspected pathway, document restoration actions.

03 / PROOF BEFORE SCALE

Built to be challenged, tested and improved.

Pre-specified performance targets for a proposed 12-month proof-of-concept; no field-performance claims are made here.

≥85%Valid data uptime

Time with usable node readings and auditable outages.

≥80%Expert agreement

Alert priority agrees with blind independent field assessment.

≥15%Fusion improvement

Relative improvement over the best single-source classifier on held-out events.

Validation design: pre/post sampling, upstream/reference controls, sensor calibration, laboratory metals, repeated biodiversity visits, wet/dry season comparisons and recorded false alerts.

04 / PRACTICAL ECONOMICS

Spend monitoring money where evidence says it matters.

Continuous screening cannot replace required compliance sampling. It can help prioritise field visits and lab tests between mandated monitoring rounds.

05 / RUSSIA–AFRICA EXPANSION

One scientific protocol. Local environmental baselines.

Start with a measurable Nigerian pilot, then co-calibrate the same field method for different geology, mining practices, ecology and climate—not one global threshold.

Plateau, Nigeria
PROPOSED PHASE 1

Six-station pilot design, mining-water pressures, ecology and verified lab confirmation.

Kuje / Abuja FCT
FIELD SCOPING

Local geological reconnaissance, minerals and quarry/sand impacts. Site-specific geology and permits before deployment.

Niger Delta
TRANSFER CONCEPT

Adapt monitoring for oil-impacted water and wetland receptors; not presented as existing mine-site data.

Russia
CO-VALIDATION PROPOSAL

Cold-weather sensors, seasonal ecology, mine-water chemistry and shared laboratory quality control.
