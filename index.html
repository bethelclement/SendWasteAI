Why AI belongs here

AI supports field decisions. It does not manufacture impact.

The system uses verified operational records as its foundation, then applies machine assistance to prioritise, detect anomalies and forecast demand.

01

Pickup prioritisation

Ranks open requests using urgency, material condition, proximity, customer commitments and service history.

02

Evidence quality checks

Flags duplicate images, unlikely weight entries, missing fields and inconsistent location records for human review.

03

Recovery forecasting

Estimates collection demand by area, customer type and material stream to improve staffing and routing.

04

Partner reporting

Converts validated transactions into traceable recovery summaries without presenting estimates as measured facts.

12-month proof plan

Credits convert directly into measurable pilot evidence.

The near-term goal is not a continent-wide claim. It is a reliable Abuja operating model with repeat use, transparent unit economics and defensible recovery data.

0–3 months

Standardise

Set verification rules, establish service baselines, configure security controls and onboard an initial collector cohort.

  • Verification completion rate
  • Median pickup response time
  • Collector onboarding completion
4–6 months

Test paid demand

Run pilots with estates, SME clusters and recovery partners while tracking repeat use and settlement accuracy.

  • Repeat collection rate
  • Paid pilot conversion
  • Cost per verified pickup
7–12 months

Prepare to scale

Publish unit economics, model performance, retention and verified recovery results before expanding geography.

  • Partner retention
  • Forecast error
  • Verified tonnes recovered
Trust is product infrastructure

Evidence, privacy and human accountability are designed in.

Human verification

Material, weight and collection completion are confirmed by authorised people, not inferred from a model alone.

Data minimisation

Only operationally necessary personal and location data should be collected, retained and shared.

Auditability

Changes to verified records, settlement status and partner reports are logged for review.

Model monitoring

Priority and anomaly models are evaluated against real outcomes, with overrides and error reporting retained.
