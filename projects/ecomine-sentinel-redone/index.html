Where to verify first

    Scientific design

    Simple enough to understand. Rigorous enough to test.

    Phase 1 uses six modular stations across impact, buffer and reference zones, paired with remote sensing and biodiversity indicators.

    01

    Measure

    Solar nodes record pH, conductivity, turbidity, water temperature, microclimate, PM and noise.

    02

    Observe

    Sentinel-2 change, bird acoustics, pollinator transects and vegetation plots show ecosystem response.

    03

    Verify

    Community reports enter only after sensor, satellite or field confirmation.

    04

    Decide

    The dashboard ranks low, moderate, high or critical risk and keeps every source visible.

    Proof-of-concept test

    The hypothesis is falsifiable.

    The fused model must outperform the strongest single-source method. If not, it must be recalibrated before scale-up.

    ≥85%data uptime
    ≥80%expert agreement
