Sensor ≠ satellite ≠ biodiversity ≠ community report

EcoMine Sentinel does not treat every signal as equivalent. Community observations are geotagged and accepted into the risk engine only after corroboration; sensor and satellite anomalies are checked against rainfall, season and reference zones before escalation.

Why Track 4

Water + ecosystems + lower footprint

Mine-water warning is the first decision layer; biodiversity and restoration evidence show whether engineering interventions are actually improving the landscape.

Why funders / operators

Measurable evidence, not ESG theatre

Each alert keeps its source evidence, every threshold is locally calibrated, and the pilot has explicit uptime, accuracy and cost targets.

Commercial path

Monitoring-as-a-service

Local assembly + installation + annual dashboard/reporting + calibration/lab services + technician training for regulators, operators and restoration contractors.

Kuje/FCT evidence basis

Expansion is proposed, not claimed as deployed

NGSA's 2022 FCT mineral-resource map lists commodities including gold, granite, feldspar, kaolin, lead-zinc, cassiterite, tantalite, tin and sand across the FCT. A 2023 study in Chibiri, Kuje documented land alteration, ecosystem disturbance, erosion and vegetation loss associated with sand mining. These justify a scoping extension after the accepted Plateau pilot - not a change to the submitted study.
